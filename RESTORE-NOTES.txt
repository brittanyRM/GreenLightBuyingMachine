ADDED AFTER THE RESTORE
=======================

app/api/public/market/route.js  (NEW)
  Read-only, no authentication. Returns PadSplit ZIP rates and city
  research so the standalone calculator can show real numbers to
  someone who is not a user of the system. Fields are listed
  explicitly rather than "*", so a column added to either table later
  cannot leak through by accident. Nothing deal-specific: no prices,
  addresses, contacts or assignments.

public/buyer-calculator.html  (UPDATED)
  - Proper HTML document. It had no DOCTYPE, no <head> and no charset,
    so served as a page the em dashes rendered as "a EUR".
  - Market panel: pick a city to load its research, pick a ZIP to
    apply that area's shared and private weekly rates and occupancy.
  - Market research panel: population, growth, households, median
    income, median age, renter share, with the recorded date.
  - PadSplit rates by ZIP map: circle size is active units, colour is
    the shared weekly rate. Click a circle to load that ZIP.
  - Visual pass: shadowed cards, accent rules on headings, KPI tiles
    lift on hover, gradient masthead.

  All three panels are optional. If /api/public/market is unreachable
  or Leaflet fails to load, they stay hidden and the calculator works
  exactly as before on its defaults.

BUYER SEES THE DEAL, THEN SELECTS THE EVIDENCE
==============================================

On arrival a buyer gets the deal and nothing else:

    the pro forma          always renders, not switchable
    Summary                the deal in one screen
    Flyer                  photos, specs, floor plan, finishes

Everything else is a tile they turn on, one click each:

    Comps                  recent sales near this house
    Map                    the house and the PadSplit ZIPs around it
    Market research        city demographics, jobs, incomes
    PadSplit market        ZIP room rates and occupancy
    Diligence              documents and assumptions
    Syndication            entitlement gated per firm

Comps used to be lit on arrival. It is not any more — a buyer opens
the link to see the deal, and the supporting evidence is there to be
asked for rather than arriving as a wall of everything at once.

Selections live in the URL, so a buyer who forwards the link to a
partner or a lender sends the view they were looking at.

  Where a buyer sees this:  /buyers/<slug>   (logged-in buyer portal)
                            /s/<token>       (share link)

MIGRATION — RUN THIS
====================

  supabase/migrations/040_map_and_research_sections.sql

Existing buyer firms have their allowed sections stored on
buyer_orgs.enabled_views. Without this migration the two new tiles are
filtered out server-side and render for nobody, which looks exactly
like the feature not working.

The migration is additive: a firm that had 'comps' was already being
shown the map, so it gets 'map'; a firm that had 'padsplit' was
already being shown the research, so it gets 'research'. Nobody loses
a section. It also drops 'numbers', since the pro forma always renders
now and an entitlement for it means nothing.

Run it in the Supabase SQL editor. Check and rollback statements are
in the comments at the bottom of the file.
