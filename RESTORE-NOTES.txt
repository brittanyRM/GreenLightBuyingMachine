ADDED AFTER THE RESTORE
=======================

app/api/public/market/route.js  (NEW)
  Read-only, no authentication. Returns PadSplit ZIP rates and city
  research so the standalone calculator can show real numbers to
  someone who is not a user of the system. Fields are listed
  explicitly rather than "*", so a column added to either table later
  cannot leak through by accident. Nothing deal-specific: no prices,
  addresses, contacts or assignments.

public/buyer-calculator.html  (UPDATED)
  - Proper HTML document. It had no DOCTYPE, no <head> and no charset,
    so served as a page the em dashes rendered as "a EUR".
  - Market panel: pick a city to load its research, pick a ZIP to
    apply that area's shared and private weekly rates and occupancy.
  - Market research panel: population, growth, households, median
    income, median age, renter share, with the recorded date.
  - PadSplit rates by ZIP map: circle size is active units, colour is
    the shared weekly rate. Click a circle to load that ZIP.
  - Visual pass: shadowed cards, accent rules on headings, KPI tiles
    lift on hover, gradient masthead.

  All three panels are optional. If /api/public/market is unreachable
  or Leaflet fails to load, they stay hidden and the calculator works
  exactly as before on its defaults.
