      Room revenue stack
      Income and expenses
      Net performance
      Capital required
      Down payment options

  basis, gallery, floor plan, comps, map, PadSplit market, market
  research, syndication, diligence.

  Previously "What's included", the scenario basis and the photo
  gallery sat above the numbers and pushed them down the page. The
  room revenue stack was tied to the Summary tile even though it is
  the top of the income statement; it is part of the pro forma now.
