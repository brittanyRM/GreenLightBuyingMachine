BUYER SHEET REMOVED — what to do
=================================

STEP 1 — DELETE THESE TWO FILES IN GITHUB
    app/buyer-sheets/page.jsx
    app/buyer-sheets/[slug]/page.jsx

  Deleting both removes the folder. Open each file → ⋯ menu →
  Delete file → Commit.

  Uploading never deletes. If you skip this the route stays live.

STEP 2 — UPLOAD THE FOLDERS IN THIS ZIP
    app/  components/  lib/  next.config.js

WHAT WAS REMOVED
  • The /buyer-sheets route, both pages
  • "Buyer sheets" from the top nav
  • The "Buyer sheet" tab from the deal page
  • The /proforma-club redirects (they pointed at the deleted route)
  • The admin interest link and the interest notification email now
    point at /deals/<slug> instead

  The deal's Pro forma tab is now the only pro forma you work in.

WHAT WAS *NOT* REMOVED, AND WHY
  components/ClubProForma.jsx and lib/proformaClub*.js are still
  there. They are not only the buyer sheet — they also render:

    app/buyers/[slug]/page.jsx   the buyer portal (logged-in buyers)
    app/s/[token]/page.jsx       every share link already sent
    app/api/buyer/deals/route.js the cash-flow figures on the
                                 buyer's deal list

  Deleting them takes down the buyer-facing product and dark-links
  every sheet already sent to a buyer. They stay until the buyer
  portal and share links are ported onto components/ProForma.jsx —
  which is a separate job, not a delete.

  Nothing internal points at them now, so they are out of your way.
