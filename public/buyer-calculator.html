What this model does not include. It assumes houses like this one keep being available to buy, that financing stays available on these terms, and that occupancy holds as the portfolio grows. It carries no vacancy event, no interest rate change, no management drag at scale, and no tax. Reinvestment is funded only by rent — no refinancing and no cash-out. Compounding makes small changes in any of these move the end figure a long way. Treat the later years as a shape, not a forecast.
